**LLM Assessment:**
ELY.pos is remarkably well-positioned for its target environment — a Philippine produce store or neighborhood retail counter operating on an iPad. The typography (Poppins), peso formatting (`₱`), and 3-column operational layout give it a distinctive identity far removed from boilerplate SaaS templates. ## Priority Issues

### [P0] Missing Cash Tender & Sukli (Change) Calculator
- **Why it matters:** In Philippine wet markets and neighborhood stores, cash is the predominant payment method. Currently, tapping "Complete Order" records the transaction without asking how much cash the customer handed over. Cashiers must mentally calculate change or use a separate physical calculator, causing checkout bottlenecks and cash register discrepancies.
- **Fix:** Introduce a high-contrast Cash Tender Sheet or Slide-Over when "Complete Order" is pressed. Display quick Philippine Peso bill buttons (`Exact`, `+₱20`, `+₱50`, `+₱100`, `+₱500`, `+₱1,000`), a touch numpad, and an unmistakable, bold Sukli readout (e.g., `Sukli: ₱355.00`) before final sale confirmation.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P0] In-Card Numeric Inputs Trigger iOS Virtual Keyboard
- **Why it matters:** Editing quantities directly in the product card uses native numeric inputs. On an iPad, focusing an `<input type="number">` raises the virtual keyboard, occluding 50% of the screen, obscuring the cart, and forcing cashiers to dismiss the keyboard after every adjustment.
- **Fix:** Replace inline text fields with tactile 44×44px stepper buttons (`+` / `-`) on catalog cards, and provide a dedicated on-screen touch numpad dialog for bulk produce weigh-ins (e.g., entering `3.25 kg`).
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] Instant Irreversible Checkout Without Undo Window
- **Why it matters:** Once the checkout button is tapped, the cart clears and the order is written to history immediately. An accidental double-tap during a busy rush can commit an unfinished order or duplicate a transaction with no quick way to revert.
- **Fix:** Implement a 5-second non-blocking toast banner (`"Order #1042 Completed · [Undo]"`) that restores cart state if tapped, paired with button debouncing during transaction processing.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Visual Catalog Homogeneity Across Fresh Produce
- **Why it matters:** With dozens of produce items on display, cards currently rely on uniform white backgrounds and small text badges. At counter distance, leafy greens, root vegetables, and fruits blend together visually, slowing down cashier scanning speed.
- **Fix:** Introduce subtle category color chips or tinted badge tokens (e.g., Emerald for Leafy Greens, Amber for Fruits, Ochre for Root Crops) and prominent stock status badges ("Low Stock", "Out of Stock").
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable colorize`

### [P3] Slop Antipatterns in CSS
- **Why it matters:** The detector flagged a 12px solid left border on `.history-card` (`side-tab`), an overshoot curve `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` on `.nav-icon` (`bounce-easing`), and an animating `width` on `.dash-seller-bar-fill` (`layout-transition`). These create minor layout thrashing and an unrefined aesthetic.
- **Fix:** Replace the 12px side border with clean card dividers and status badges, standardize transition easing to smooth deceleration `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, and animate progress bars via `transform: scaleX()`.
- **Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`
